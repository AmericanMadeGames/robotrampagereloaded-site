Gameplay

Walk right. Wreck everything.

No puzzles and no stress. The fun is in the wrecking, and the game rewards it with combo multipliers, scrap and plenty of screen shake.

Floor by floor

Level the city. Literally.

Cut a building at the base and the whole thing comes down. Hit it higher and you take off the top. Concrete crumbles, wood creaks, glass shatters and metal groans as it goes.

  • Aim the eye laser anywhere on screen
  • Punch shells and missiles straight back at the army
  • Jetpack up and stomp down on whole squads
  • Chain a fuel tank into a block-wide explosion
Ultimate

The military brought tanks. You brought a nuke.

Leveling buildings and taking down enemies charges the nuke meter. When it's full you'll hear a siren and a warhead whistle, then a white flash takes out everything on screen.

  • Charges across sectors as you wreck
  • Clears every enemy, building and incoming shot on screen
  • Full-screen flash, shockwave and mushroom cloud
Every sector

Clear it. Upgrade. Go again.

Clear a sector and the gate opens. Then you pick one of three upgrades: a faster laser, heavier plating, Quake Fist shockwaves, bigger explosions and more. Upgrades stack for the rest of the run, and the army gets tougher with every sector.

  • 11 upgrades that stack over a run
  • Rain, thunderstorms, fog and snow (lightning wrecks things too)
  • A quick training level that teaches every move

Nothing. Robot Rampage Reloaded does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, sign-ups, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't use any third-party services that collect data.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things in its own storage on your device:

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can reset your stats in Settings, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Common questions

How do I play?

Walk right and level every building in the sector to open the gate at the end. Hold your finger anywhere on screen to aim the laser. Use ‹ › to walk, ˄ to jump and fly with the jetpack, and the fist to punch. When the nuke is charged, tap ☢. You can replay the Training level from the main menu at any time.

The gate won't open

The gate opens only after every building in the sector is down. Red arrows at the edge of the screen point to buildings you've missed behind you. A building only counts once its ground floor is destroyed, so aim the laser at the base.

How do I unlock new worlds and skins?

Every run pays out scrap, even if you quit to the menu. You earn more for higher scores, reaching later levels, beating your best, unlocking achievements and your first run of the day. Spend it in the Shop to unlock new worlds and robot skins.

Do skins change how the robot plays?

No. Skins are cosmetic only, and every robot plays the same.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Settings → Reset stats & high score clears your stats, but it keeps your scrap and the items you've unlocked. Deleting the app removes everything, and there's no way to restore it, because we never store a copy.

The game feels slow on my device

Go to Settings and set Graphics to Low. That uses fewer particles and skips some effects so the game runs faster on older devices. You can also turn on Show FPS to check performance.

I can't hear anything, or I don't feel haptics

Check that Sound and Haptics are on in Settings and that your device isn't in silent mode.

The touch buttons are too small or too big

Settings → Control size lets you choose Small, Medium or Large.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 15 or later. No internet connection needed.
